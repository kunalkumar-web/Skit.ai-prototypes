Why category matters

Collections AI isn't conversational AI with a collections module

The platforms you'll compare Skit.ai against fall into two categories. Which one you choose shapes everything — from compliance posture to liquidation rates.

Purpose-built

Collections Intelligence platforms

Built from day one for regulated debt recovery. Every model, every workflow, every compliance decision is tuned for collections — not retrofitted.

  • FDCPA / TCPA / Reg F / UDAAP compliance built into the action layer
  • Promise-to-Pay detection, propensity scoring, treatment strategy
  • Mid-call language switching for India's multilingual borrower base
  • SOC2 · PCI-DSS · ISO 27001 · RBI Fair Practices Code

→ Skit.ai, Gnani.ai

General-purpose

Horizontal / enterprise CX platforms

Broad conversational AI platforms where collections is one of 10–20 supported use cases. The underlying model is not tuned for debt recovery conversations.

  • Generic NLU and dialog management
  • No collections-specific compliance documentation
  • No Promise-to-Pay or delinquency-bucket workflows out of the box
  • Enterprise CX metrics (CSAT, AHT) — not liquidation rate or PTP kept

→ Yellow.ai, Haptik, Sarvam.ai

Where Skit.ai wins

Three things no horizontal platform can replicate

These aren't features — they're the result of building a single product for a single outcome for seven years.

Compliance at the action layer

Most platforms log compliance. Skit.ai enforces it in the moment — stopping a prohibited action before it happens, not flagging it afterward. FDCPA, TCPA, Reg F, UDAAP, RBI Fair Practices Code, TRAI calling-hour limits, and DPDP Act data-principal obligations are each wired into the AI's decision tree, not a post-call audit module.

Collections-native AI models

A general conversational AI model learns from support tickets, lead-gen forms, and booking flows. Skit.ai's models are trained exclusively on debt recovery conversations — promise-to-pay negotiations, objection handling, hardship detection, and payment-plan structuring. Seven years of collections-only training produces outcomes that a fine-tuned horizontal model cannot match.

Mid-call language switching

India's borrower base doesn't stay in one language. Skit.ai detects and responds to real-time language shifts — a borrower who starts in English and moves to Telugu mid-sentence gets a seamless, uninterrupted conversation. 10+ Indian languages and 160+ dialects, switching at the word level without a restart.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Skit.ai RBI compliant for outbound collections calls in India?

Yes. Skit.ai's India deployments follow the RBI Fair Practices Code for loan recovery — calling only within TRAI-mandated windows (8 AM–7 PM), maintaining full call recordings and audit trails, respecting DND registry requirements, and adhering to DPDP Act 2023 borrower-data obligations. Compliance rules are enforced in the AI's action layer — the system will not initiate a prohibited action, not merely flag it post-call. SOC2 Type II, PCI-DSS, and ISO 27001 certifications are available for enterprise due-diligence reviews.

How does Skit.ai handle Hindi–English code-switching in collections calls?

Skit.ai's multilingual NLU engine detects mid-sentence language shifts and responds in kind — no restart, no "please repeat in English." A borrower who begins in English and moves to Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu mid-conversation gets uninterrupted service. The platform supports 10+ Indian languages and 160+ regional dialects, with real-time switching engineered specifically for India's code-switching patterns in collections contexts (e.g., borrowers switching to their native language when discussing financial distress).

Can Skit.ai integrate with our existing core banking or LMS system?

Yes. Skit.ai offers 20+ pre-built integrations including core banking systems (Finacle, Nucleus FI), contact centre platforms (Genesys, Avaya), CRMs (Salesforce), cloud telephony (Exotel, Ozonetel, Tata Communications), and cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP). API and SDK access are available for custom integrations. A typical NBFC integration takes 4–8 weeks from kickoff to live calls, including compliance configuration and UAT.

How does Skit.ai compare to Gnani.ai for Indian BFSI collections?

Gnani.ai is Skit.ai's closest India-based peer — both are purpose-built voice AI platforms with collections focus and Indian language depth. Gnani leads on public speech-accuracy benchmarks and has a strong case-study library (Bank of Baroda, Muthoot Finance, 25.6× ROI documented). Skit.ai leads on US ARM-grade compliance (FDCPA/TCPA/Reg F) for lenders with cross-border portfolios, a full end-to-end collections intelligence layer (propensity scoring, treatment strategy, PTP decisioning), and India + US deployment under a single regulatory framework. Buyers choosing between them typically run a parallel pilot and compare liquidation rates on matched portfolios.

Does Skit.ai work for MFI (microfinance) and cooperative bank collections?

Yes. Skit.ai's India deployments span banks, NBFCs, MFIs, fintechs, BNPL lenders, and ARM agencies. MFI deployments typically run in regional Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, Bengali) with empathetic conversation design suited to borrowers in financial hardship. The platform supports low-data environments for rural borrower connectivity and integrates with microfinance-specific LMS systems. Cooperative bank deployments benefit from the same RBI compliance framework with customisable calling scripts aligned to fair-practice standards.

What happens when a borrower is in genuine financial distress?

Skit.ai's AI detects distress signals in real-time — tone, language patterns, and explicit statements of hardship — and immediately adapts the conversation. Depending on your configuration, this can mean: switching to a more empathetic conversation path, offering a payment deferral or restructured plan from pre-approved options, or escalating to a human agent with full context in under 3 seconds. The system never uses escalating pressure tactics when distress is detected. This is a compliance requirement we've built into the model, not an optional feature.
