Frequently Asked Questions

What is Skit.ai and how does it work as a Voice AI platform?

Skit.ai is a Voice AI platform that automates collections, marketing and customer servicing conversations for India's banks, NBFCs, MFIs and enterprises. The platform runs outbound and inbound voice conversations in 10+ Indian languages, with compliance for RBI, DPDP and TRAI built into every call.

Does Skit.ai's Voice AI manage marketing outreach, or is it exclusively for collections?

Skit.ai runs all three on one platform: marketing conversations (loan origination, cross-sell, lead qualification, renewals), inbound servicing (FAQ handling, balance queries, ticket resolution) and collections (payment reminders, PTP capture, settlement offers, right-party verification) — so an enterprise's voice experience stays consistent from acquisition through recovery.

What inbound and servicing use cases can Skit.ai's platform handle?

Skit.ai automates FAQ handling and routing, account balance queries, application status checks, complaint handling with escalation to a human agent, and service ticket resolution — available across voice channels.

How does Skit.ai handle India's diverse languages in automated customer conversations?

Skit.ai supports 10+ regional Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali and Punjabi, with 160+ dialect variations, and can switch languages mid-call based on the customer's preference.

Is Skit.ai's platform compliant with Indian banking regulations and data security standards?

Yes. Skit.ai's platform is designed for India's regulatory bar: calling-hours discipline and right-party verification aligned to the RBI Fair Practices Code, consent capture and data-retention controls built for DPDP Act 2023 readiness, and registered-sender, consent-based outreach aligned to TRAI's commercial-communication rules. Skit.ai is also ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 certified, and SOC 2 Type II and PCI-DSS compliant.

How is Skit.ai different from a standard voice bot vendor?

Skit.ai is built specifically for regulated, high-stakes conversations in BFSI — every call is logged to an immutable, audit-ready trail with AI disclosure by default, and voice flows are tuned to each sub-vertical's risk profile rather than using one generic conversational script across industries.

What systems can Skit.ai integrate with?

Skit.ai integrates with contact-centre and dialer platforms (Genesys, Avaya, Cisco), CRMs (Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics), cloud telephony (Exotel, Ozonetel, Tata Communications), cloud hosting (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and helpdesk tools (Freshdesk, Zendesk) — without requiring a rip-and-replace of existing infrastructure.
