Skit.ai is a Voice AI platform that automates collections, marketing and customer servicing conversations for India’s banks, NBFCs, MFIs and enterprises. The platform runs outbound and inbound voice conversations in 10–12 Indian languages, trained across 160+ dialect variations, on both the NLP and LLM platforms.

Skit.ai runs all three on one platform: marketing conversations (loan origination, cross-sell, lead qualification, renewals), inbound servicing (FAQ handling, balance queries, ticket resolution) and collections (payment reminders, PTP capture, settlement offers) — so an enterprise’s voice experience stays consistent from acquisition through recovery.

Skit.ai automates FAQ handling and routing, account balance queries, application status checks, complaint handling with escalation to a human agent, and service ticket resolution — available across voice channels.

Skit.ai supports 10–12 Indian languages: Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Odia, Bengali, Punjabi and Assamese, trained across 160+ dialect variations, on both the NLP and LLM platforms. Coverage is built for collections-specific phrasing rather than claiming a language competitors do not support.

Skit.ai holds ISO 27001, ISO 42001, SOC 2 Type II and PCI-DSS. Calling-hour monitoring and empathetic recovery conversation design support RBI collections practice in part. DPDP processor duties are being implemented ahead of phased commencement around May 2027. TRAI DLT and preference checks are in the India calling rollout; 1600-series migration with telephony partners is in progress, not complete.

Skit.ai is built for regulated BFSI conversations — collections-specific dialect depth (160+ variations), production scale with large Indian banking clients, and two-way CRM write-back of dispositions, recordings and summaries — rather than a generic script reused across industries.
