Frequently Asked Questions

What marketing use cases can Skit.ai's Voice AI support?

Skit.ai's India site lists loan origination and top-ups, product cross-sell, lead qualification, renewal and retention calls, and savings campaign outreach. The platform uses GenAI-powered voice agents to automate outbound conversations at scale.

How does Skit.ai qualify a marketing lead during a call?

The AI voice agent listens to the customer's responses during the live conversation, detects intent in real time, qualifies interested users, and can hot-transfer high-intent users to a sales agent.

What security and compliance controls does Skit.ai provide for enterprise deployments?

Skit.ai's platform is built for regulated, enterprise-grade deployments, with controls including ISO 27001, PCI-DSS and SOC 2, and audit-ready call transcripts. Exact compliance requirements are confirmed with each client's legal and compliance team before go-live.

What languages can Skit.ai's Voice AI use in India?

Skit.ai's India site says the platform supports 10+ regional Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali and Punjabi, with 160+ dialect variations. It also states that the bot can switch languages during a call.

Can Skit.ai integrate with our existing CRM and banking systems?

Yes. Skit.ai's India site lists integrations across contact-centre platforms, CRMs, lending systems, cloud and telephony, with API-based data flow into existing workflows. Exact integration scope depends on the client's stack.
