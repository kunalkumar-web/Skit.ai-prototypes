Frequently Asked Questions

Is Skit.ai RBI and TRAI compliant for outbound calls in India?

Yes. Skit.ai's India deployments follow the RBI Fair Practices Code — calling only within TRAI-mandated windows (8 AM–7 PM), maintaining full call recordings and audit trails, respecting DND registry requirements, and adhering to DPDP Act 2023 data-principal obligations. Compliance rules are enforced in the AI's action layer — the system will not initiate a prohibited action, not merely flag it after the fact. SOC2 Type II, PCI-DSS, and ISO 27001 certifications are available for enterprise due-diligence reviews.

How does Skit.ai handle Hindi–English code-switching on customer calls?

Skit.ai's multilingual NLU engine detects mid-sentence language shifts and responds in kind — no restart, no "please repeat in English." A customer who begins in English and moves to Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu mid-conversation gets uninterrupted service. The platform supports 10+ Indian languages and 160+ regional dialects, with real-time switching built for how Indian customers actually speak — across banking, lending and insurance conversations alike.

Can Skit.ai integrate with our existing core banking or LMS system?

Yes. Skit.ai offers 20+ pre-built integrations including core banking systems (Finacle, Nucleus FI), contact centre platforms (Genesys, Avaya), CRMs (Salesforce), cloud telephony (Exotel, Ozonetel, Tata Communications), and cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP). API and SDK access are available for custom integrations. A typical NBFC integration takes 4–8 weeks from kickoff to live calls, including compliance configuration and UAT.

What industries and use cases does Skit.ai support in India?

Skit.ai serves banks, NBFCs, fintech lenders and insurers across the full customer lifecycle — KYC and onboarding, payment reminders and collections, policy renewals, claims status updates, and cross-sell — on voice, SMS, email and chat. The same platform that runs a payment reminder can also handle a policy renewal call or a claims update, so BFSI teams don't need a separate vendor for every workflow.

Does Skit.ai work for MFI (microfinance) and cooperative bank use cases?

Yes. Skit.ai's India deployments span banks, NBFCs, MFIs, fintechs, and BNPL lenders. MFI deployments typically run in regional Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, Bengali) with empathetic conversation design suited to customers in financial hardship. The platform supports low-data environments for rural connectivity and integrates with microfinance-specific LMS systems. Cooperative bank deployments benefit from the same RBI compliance framework with customisable calling scripts aligned to fair-practice standards.

What happens when a customer is in genuine financial distress?

Skit.ai's AI detects distress signals in real-time — tone, language patterns, and explicit statements of hardship — and immediately adapts the conversation. Depending on your configuration, this can mean: switching to a more empathetic conversation path, offering a payment deferral or restructured plan from pre-approved options, or escalating to a human agent with full context in under 3 seconds. The system never uses escalating pressure tactics when distress is detected. This is a compliance requirement we've built into the model, not an optional feature.
