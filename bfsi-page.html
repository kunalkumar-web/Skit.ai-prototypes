India Regulatory & Security Architecture

Built for India's BFSI Regulatory Bar from the First Ring

India has no FDCPA equivalent — enterprise trust rests on TRAI calling rules, DLT registration, RBI expectations for outbound and recovery conduct, the DPDP Act 2023, and Tier-1 bank infosec standards.

No Sensitive Data Capture

The voice agent never asks for, captures, or stores OTPs, MPINs, internet banking passwords, or full card numbers. Complete defense against credential theft by design.

TRAI & DLT Messaging Compliance

Any follow-up SMS or payment link dispatches exclusively through bank-approved DLT templates with verified sender headers and hash IDs. Blind unapproved messaging is blocked.

Calling-Hours & Recording Disclosure

Outbound calls run strictly within TRAI-mandated calling hours (9 AM to 8 PM) on bank telemarketing series (140/160), with mandatory recording disclosure immediately after right-party confirmation.

RBI Conduct & Recovery Expectations

Tone and cadence align strictly to RBI Fair Practices Code for loan recovery. Harassment-free, empathy-first conversations with automatic escalation of disputes and financial hardship.

DPDP Act & Indian Data Residency

100% customer data residency within India. Full adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 with cryptographic consent logging and purpose limitation controls.

Enterprise Bank InfoSec & Dedicated VPC

Skit has cleared rigorous information security audits from India's largest private and public banks that smaller vendors cannot pass. Supports dedicated single-tenant VPC deployments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is voice AI for banking?

GenAI voice agents that call or answer bank customers and hold a natural, two-way conversation to sell, service or recover, in the customer's language, at scale.

How do Indian banks use voice AI?

For outbound campaigns (pre-approved loans, credit-card and cross-sell), collections across delinquency buckets, and inbound servicing and agent assist.

Is Skit.ai's voice AI compliant with Indian regulations?

Calls run on approved telemarketing numbers within TRAI calling hours with a recording disclosure, SMS uses approved DLT templates, and the agent never captures OTPs, MPINs, passwords or full card numbers. Data is handled to bank information-security standards.

What languages does the voice AI support?

Hindi, English and Hinglish today, with regional languages including Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali and Marathi.

Can a voice bot handle collections and loan journeys?

Yes. It qualifies intent, handles objections, guides multi-step digital journeys, and hands off to an agent or branch when a person is needed.

How is voice AI different from a traditional IVR?

An IVR reads a fixed menu. A GenAI voice agent understands what the customer says, answers in context, and adapts the conversation.

What results can a bank expect from moving to GenAI voice?

On live production data, moving from NLP to LLM lifted connectivity and engagement, produced more qualified leads per lead dialled, and reduced dials per lead. Exact figures depend on the use case and base.
