Frequently asked questions

BFSI Voice AI: common questions from Indian banks & lenders.

Straight answers on coverage, compliance, languages and deployment speed for banks, NBFCs, MFIs and lenders evaluating Skit.ai.

Does Skit.ai's Voice AI work for banks, NBFCs and MFIs in India?

Yes. Skit.ai is a Voice AI platform built for India's BFSI sector, covering banks, NBFCs, MFIs, lenders and BNPL providers across marketing, servicing and collections conversations. Deployments are configured to each sub-vertical's risk profile, regulatory obligations and customer language.

Is Skit.ai compliant with RBI, DPDP and TRAI regulations?

Skit.ai's platform is designed to support India's regulatory bar: calling-hours discipline and right-party verification aligned to the RBI Fair Practices Code, consent capture and data-retention controls built for DPDP Act 2023 readiness, and registered-sender, consent-based outreach aligned to TRAI's commercial-communication rules. Skit.ai is also ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 certified, and SOC 2 Type II and PCI-DSS compliant.

What BFSI use cases does Skit.ai automate?

Skit.ai automates conversations across the BFSI lifecycle: loan origination and top-ups, cross-sell and lead qualification in marketing; FAQ handling, balance queries and VKYC/Re-KYC verification in servicing; and payment reminders, PTP capture, settlement offers and right-party verification in collections.

What languages does Skit.ai support for Indian BFSI customers?

Skit.ai supports 10+ regional Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali and Punjabi, and can switch languages mid-call based on the customer's preference.

What systems can Skit.ai integrate with in a bank or NBFC's tech stack?

Skit.ai integrates with contact-centre and dialer platforms, CRMs and loan-management systems, cloud hosting providers, and telephony (SIP, WebRTC, PSTN) commonly used by Indian banks and NBFCs — without requiring a rip-and-replace of existing infrastructure.

How fast can a bank or NBFC go live with Skit.ai?

Skit.ai's BFSI deployments can go live in under 48 hours using pre-built voice flows for common banking, NBFC and lending use cases, with 125+ deployments completed to date.

Which Indian banks and lenders use Skit.ai?

Skit.ai is trusted by 100+ enterprises, including named engagements such as Axis Bank, alongside other leading banks and NBFCs. Skit.ai's platform has driven ₹600Cr+ in total collections at an 82% liquidation rate, and placed 100M+ voice calls.

Is Skit.ai's use of AI disclosed to customers during a call?

Yes. Skit.ai discloses AI usage by default on every call, and every conversation is logged to an immutable, audit-ready trail — the same disclosure-by-design approach Skit.ai runs for FDCPA-regulated US collections, applied to India's BFSI compliance bar.
