Frequently Asked Questions

The questions buyers actually ask before signing. If yours isn’t here, ping us — we’ll add it.

What is Skit.ai and how does its AI debt collection work?

Skit.ai is an AI debt collection platform that helps companies collect outstanding payments using AI agents that talk to customers like real people. The AI reaches customers across calls, messages, and other channels, handles routine follow-ups, and knows when to hand things over to a human. Teams stay in control, customers are treated with empathy, and businesses get paid faster without adding more staff.

Does Skit.ai have real-world debt collection experience?

Absolutely. Collections isn’t a feature of our platform — it’s the foundation it’s built on.

Skit has partnered with 53,000+ creditors across 19+ debt types, powering recovery for banks, agencies, fintechs, healthcare systems, and utilities. Our AI is trained on millions of regulated consumer interactions, incorporating the language, tone, and negotiation patterns unique to collections.

Every model operates within our Compliance Layer, which aligns with FDCPA, TCPA, Reg F, and state-level rules, ensuring performance never comes at the cost of regulatory safety.

With over eight years of live deployments, one billion conversations, and $1B+ in accounts resolved, Skit.ai brings the experience, data, and compliance depth needed to deliver results from day one.

What makes Skit.ai different from other debt collection software?

Unlike traditional debt collection software, Skit.ai works like a filter between your customers and your team. Our AI handles the high-volume, everyday follow-ups across channels, and passes only the complex or sensitive cases to your collectors. That means your team focuses on what really matters, customers get consistent and respectful conversations, and you collect more without relying on agencies or adding headcount.

Does Skit.ai offer a pilot before full deployment?

Yes. Every engagement begins with a live, production-grade pilot designed to validate performance, compliance, and ROI in real-world conditions.
These 30–60 day pilots use your actual portfolios and systems, with full integration, compliance alignment, and live reporting, not simulations or test data.
From the start, clients have visibility into key metrics like right-party contact, promise-to-pay, and cost per resolution, tracked through transparent dashboards.
The goal is simple: prove measurable impact quickly and give your team complete confidence before scaling across the organization.

How does Skit.ai ensure FDCPA, TCPA, and Reg F compliance?

Skit.ai ensures debt collection compliance by building the rules directly into the system, so it automatically follows laws like FDCPA, TCPA, and Reg F. It checks compliance before, during, and after each call, records and securely stores interactions with audit trails, and enforces safeguards like opt-outs, call timing limits, and disclaimers. It also uses strong security standards (SOC 2, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001) to protect data, and updates its rules regularly as regulations change.

What kind of results can you expect from a pilot?

Most clients see measurable results within the first 30–60 days:

  • Higher right-party contact (RPC) and promise-to-pay (PTP) and recovery rates.
  • More call handling capacity for your agents
  • Consistent consumer experience

Our pilots are fully instrumented with live dashboards, tracking performance in real time so you can quantify impact before committing anything.

Can Skit.ai integrate with your existing debt collection systems and workflows?

Absolutely. Skit.ai is designed to fit into your existing tech stack, not replace it. It integrates easily with CRMs (Salesforce, Temenos, Finvi, etc.), dialers (Dialpad, NICE, etc.), and payment processors (Repay, Stripe, etc.). You can connect through secure APIs, middleware, or SFTP, with real-time or batch updates.

All call dispositions, payments, and consumer updates sync back to your system of record. We also provide sandbox and reconciliation tools to ensure your data remains consistent and auditable across systems.

How quickly can you go live with Skit.ai?

Standard deployments go live in 4–6 weeks, using flat-file or common CRM and telephony integrations. Complex enterprise integrations typically take 3–4 months. Every rollout starts with scoping and compliance alignment, followed by sandbox testing and production go-live.

Standard integrations use JSON-based APIs or SFTP templates, making setup straightforward. Our team handles mapping, QA, and go-live support, ensuring a smooth transition with no downtime to existing operations.

Is our data used to train shared AI models?

Not in any way that identifies you. Our standard models learn only from anonymized, aggregated patterns across clients, and training uses synthetic, PII-abstracted transcripts rather than raw recordings. If you need full isolation, we train a dedicated model exclusively on your data, with no cross-client sharing, and can host it in your own VPC.

Models trained for you are your property and are deleted within 30 days of contract end.

What human oversight exists for disputes and hardship cases?

The AI handles disputes only within its trained regulatory scope (FDCPA and applicable state rules) and logs every one. Anything outside that scope is escalated to a live agent within one or two exchanges, with the full context attached.

A consumer who is distressed, including any sign of self-harm risk, is never disconnected: the AI responds with empathy and transfers to a person. Frustration is detected through sentiment analysis and can trigger escalation too. If a consumer asks for a human, the AI either transfers immediately or makes one attempt to help first, depending on your settings. Every threshold is configurable to your compliance policy.

Does the AI tell consumers they’re speaking with an AI?

Yes. Every call opens with a disclosure such as “Hi, this is [Name], an AI assistant calling from [Company].” We apply it on all calls as standard practice, not only where it’s required, because more states are adopting disclosure rules. Utah and Colorado currently have explicit requirements, and the wording is configurable to your compliance policy.

Which languages does Skit.ai support?

For US calling, English and Spanish, with the ability to switch languages mid-call. Globally the platform supports 46+ languages, including 10+ Indian languages.

Where is our data stored, and for how long?

On AWS, inside your region: US customer data stays in the US, and India customer data stays in India. You can choose a single-tenant (dedicated) or multi-tenant (logically separated) deployment.

Retention is configurable. Call recordings are typically kept for up to 5 years, with options to extend or export, and data received through SFTP or CSV batches is purged within 30 days.

Can we choose the AI’s voice?

Yes. Choose from 35–50+ preset voices by gender, tone and persona, and switch voice per campaign, for example a softer tone for early-stage delinquency and a firmer one for later stages. We also support custom voice cloning if you want one consistent brand voice across collections and service.

What happens if part of the system goes down?

Outreach keeps running. The platform runs on multi-node, auto-scaling AWS infrastructure with active-active redundancy, multiple speech-recognition and LLM providers, and multiple telephony carriers, so a single component failure triggers automatic failover rather than a pause. It is backed by a 99.5% uptime SLA.
